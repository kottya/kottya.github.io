## Innovator Journal 1

### Process of Deciding on the Project

   My original idea of my project was creating a Webb specific sports analytics platform. However, as I started working on the project on the first few classes, i noticed that my idea heavily overlapped with one of my classmates. My data on my laptop also got lost because of an accident, so I decided to start a new project over.

### New Theme of My Project

I decided to choose earthquake as a theme for my new project. I have been interested in it as a citizens of a earthquake prone country. I have been taught a lot about its effect on the society, and I have also been through one of the biggest earthquakes of Japanese/world history: The Great East Japan Earthquake. Earthquakes are natural phenomenon and are inevitable when living in Japan, and I wanted to learn more about how it works and what technology can do to minimize the damage from it.

My first focus in this project will be on architecture related technology.
